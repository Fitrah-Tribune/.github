# 📰 Fitrah Tribune

> **By Students. For Everyone.**

Fitrah Tribune is the official student news community of **Fitrah Islamic World Academy (FIWA)**, dedicated to documenting school life, celebrating achievements, and sharing stories that matter.

Our mission is to provide accurate, engaging, and student-driven journalism while fostering creativity, responsibility, and collaboration.

---

## 🌟 What We Do

* 🗞️ Publish school news and event coverage
* 📸 Share photography and multimedia content
* ✍️ Write articles, interviews, and editorials
* 💡 Build digital tools and platforms for our publication
* 🤝 Encourage students to develop communication and leadership skills

---

## 🚀 Projects

### 🌐 Fitrah Tribune Website

Our modern website is built with:

* ⚛️ React
* ⚡ Vite
* 🎨 Tailwind CSS
* ☁️ Cloudflare Pages

The website provides a fast and accessible platform for readers to explore articles, announcements, and school events.
